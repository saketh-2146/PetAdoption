# Privacy Policy

Welcome to PetConnect's Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect personal information such as your name, email address, mobile number, and physical address when you register and create a profile on PetConnect.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
Your information is used to facilitate pet adoption processes, verify users, and provide a personalized experience. 

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Information may be shared with verified shelters or adopters solely for the purpose of completing an adoption.

## 4. Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@petconnect.com.
